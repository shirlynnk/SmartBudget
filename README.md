# Theme: Financial Inclusion and Literacy

## Problem Statement
Many people, especially students, young professionals and others living on a fixed monthly income or allowance, spend money in small, frequent amounts and lose track of how much of their budget is left, so their money often runs out before the month ends. Poor tracking leads to overspending, borrowing, missed bills, stress and an inability to save, while knowing your spending early gives you a chance to change it. Currently, most people rely on memory, notebooks, spreadsheets or phone notes, or check mobile money and bank statements after the money has already left. These methods are easy to lose or get wrong, slow to update on a phone, not linked to a budget, and give no warning when users near their limit, no grouping by category and no reminder to record expenses.
## Solution
SmartBudget is an Android app that addresses these gaps. Users set and edit a monthly budget, record expenses by category, and see their status on a dashboard showing budget, total spent and amount remaining. The app also sends a daily reminder to record spending and offers an exchange rate screen for foreign-currency purchases. Because data is stored on the phone, it works without an internet connection.
